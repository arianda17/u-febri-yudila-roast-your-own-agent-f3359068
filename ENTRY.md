# Entry

**Line:**
> My agent's wrong answers ship with confidence; its apologies ship on repeat.

**Character count:** 76

## Why it wins

1. **It roasts the entrant's own agent** — first person ("My agent"), not a generic chatbot jab at some faceless bot.
2. **Twist that pays off** — the setup sounds like shipping competence; the punch reveals that the only thing shipping reliably is the apology. The last word, "repeat," is the punchline: an apology loop with no fix in sight.
3. **Specific to AI agents** — confidently wrong answers paired with reflexive apologies is classic LLM-agent behavior; a person or a toaster doesn't hallucinate correctness and then apologize on a schedule.
4. **Fresh** — not the overused "hallucinates facts" or "says sorry a lot" tropes; the framing is a shipping pipeline that only ships regret.
5. **Tight** — 76 characters, every word earns its place: *wrong, confidence, apologies, repeat*. No filler, no caption needed.
